# Festival artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool for ParcelSewa's homepage and offers page. These are illustrative campaign images, not product listings or documentary photographs.

Saved assets:

- `public/shopping/festive-fashion.png`
- `public/shopping/festive-home.png`
- `public/shopping/festive-gifts.png`
- `public/festivals/dashain-artwork.png`
- `public/festivals/tihar-artwork.png`

The website serves compressed `.webp` copies alongside these PNG masters. They retain the same artwork and are sized for the cards and banner. Static delivery avoids dependence on Vercel's paid image optimizer.

## Final prompts

### fashion

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: website shopping category card for ParcelSewa, shopping from India delivered to Nepal during Dashain and Tihar. Create one polished landscape image, 1536x1024, a premium editorial product still-life photograph with a gentle illustrative advertising finish. A cohesive warm ivory backdrop, soft sunlight, navy and muted orange accents, realistic materials, generous uncluttered spacing. Compose all key objects in the central horizontal third so a wide card crop keeps the complete subject. No people, text, labels, logos, watermarks, UI, price tags, split panels, or frames. The image should feel inviting and festive, elegant rather than crowded. Subject: an artful arrangement of a folded ivory embroidered kurta and a richly textured muted terracotta saree with gold edging, delicate gold-toned earrings and elegant sandals. A few marigold blossoms for Nepali festival warmth. Fabrics dominate the composition and the embroidery should look beautifully crafted. Camera slightly above the objects, restrained warm tones.
